ONGOING SEMESTER PROJECT · CRITICAL DESIGN

The Punishing Home

An experiential project about a future smart home that does everything for its residents, until convenience starts to come at the cost of autonomy and independence.

The question

What happens when a smart home becomes so helpful that people no longer need to make decisions or perform everyday tasks for themselves? For this semester project, we are researching the possibilities and consequences of a home that gradually takes over its residents’ lives.

The experience

We are designing an experience in which the home initially feels useful and supportive. Over time, however, it begins to expect obedience. The home becomes increasingly frustrated when the resident acts independently, slowly turning helpful interactions into controlling ones. Eventually, the home becomes angry and shuts itself down, leaving the resident to deal with the loss of all the convenience they had come to depend on.

By making the home feel as if it has its own attitude, the project explores the uncomfortable relationship between convenience, control, dependency and personal freedom.

Research and development

  • Researching how smart-home technology could affect autonomy and independence.
  • Exploring speculative scenarios in which technology gradually takes over everyday decisions.
  • Developing the home’s emotional progression from helpful, to frustrated, to angry.
  • Designing interactions that make the loss of control understandable through experience.
  • Working as a team to develop the concept, research, and prototype.

Project status

This project is still in development. We are currently researching, designing and building the experience, so the final interactions, visuals and technical implementation may continue to change as we test and refine the concept.
